# Fugue I in C minor, v5: v3 with the chromaticism softened

Same piece as v3: same countersubject, form, canons (augmentation canon b13–16, inversion canon b19–24), rhythm, peak stretto (b27–28), final stretto over a tonic pedal (b31–32), and a minor close. Length is 2:01. The prelude is v3 and unchanged (copied here). v4 has been discarded.

## Subject edit (one note)
v3: `C5 B4 D5 **Ab4** Eb5 G4 F5 Eb D C D Eb D C B C G`
v5: `C5 B4 D5 **G4** Eb5 G4 F5 …` (same rhythm and contour)

The D→Ab tritone and the B…Ab diminished-7th leap are gone. The subject now pivots on the dominant G (tonic/dominant zigzag). B (leading tone) is kept as its one "colour" note. The answer (the inversion) and every later entry change correspondingly, by one note each: b3, b9, b13/14, b27, b31/32.

## Other changes
- **E1 (b6):** Db5→Bb4. The generator's pitch set is diatonic C minor (no Db/C#), so the bass is now Bb–G–Bb–C.
- **b7:** one countersubject note follows the new inversion.
- **b15–16:** the free voice is diatonic G minor (no Ab or Db).
- **E3 (b17–18):** regenerated from Eb major plus A only, a clean descending sequence Gm–Cm–F–Bb–Eb–Ab. No F#/E/Db clashes.
- **b19:** two bass notes.
- **b24.3–29:** hand-rewritten (these were the weak spots).
  - Lead-in `C5 Ab4 Bb4 C5 G4`.
  - Build b25–26: a lament-style bass `C Bb Ab G | F F# G G-F` (i–v6–iv6–V–iv–vii°7/V–V–V7). The upper voice climbs in parallel 3rds/10ths through broken chords, accelerating to D6–G6.
  - Peak b27–28: the subject at the top is unchanged. The lower voice keeps the inverted answer's head, then turns into a cadential bass `C B C Ab G` (this removed the stretto parallels and unprepared clashes).
  - b29: a deceptive cadence onto Ab with Eb6 on top (it was a G6 clash), then a clean Neapolitan Db arpeggio.

## Self-critique
- The inversion canon (b19–24) is untouched apart from b19. It still contains F-minor Db/E colour and is the most "academic" stretch.
- The inverted answer is inherently less key-defining than a tonal answer would be. This was kept because the form relies on it.
- The b25–26 build is now plain and functional. It may sound a little textbook next to the canon.
